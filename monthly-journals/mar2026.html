Math final

I like to think that I learned a lot about myself during this process. I learned that studying is always better with a friend. I learned that working out problems on a (preferably large) whiteboard is the best way to learn. I learned that Stokes’ Theorem FREAKING SUCKS.

I also learned how to give up.

Yes, this is probably the most important thing I’ve learned from self-studying multivariable calculus. Instead of brute forcing my way through questions I didn’t know how to do, I would pause, take a break, and come back to it later (or not). I would give up on doing things by myself and choose to ask others for help instead.

So after 3 hours of scribbling on one small whiteboard while wearing my lucky red sweater (indicator of how badly I think an exam will go), I was done; free, finally. Except my credits might not transfer. I’ll update you guys when I have to re-take this class next year…

Goals for Quarter 4 (non-academic)

  • SLEEP! MORE! Self-explanatory, but I was recently reading about all the long-term effects of chronic sleep deprivation. Apparently, you can’t recover sleep debt by sleeping more on the weekends??? I fear I have accumulated enough sleep debt to last for the next two decades…
  • Learn how to learn efficiently. I may know how to study, but this doesn’t equate to optimized learning. Maybe this is more of a lifelong goal, but it’s still an important skill to practice before I go to college.
  • Tap into my inner whimsy. Side-quest galore. I’m talking about next-level grass-touching (needed after the last 3 quarters).
  • Dorm cooking. Aka cooking with no stove, no oven, no refrigerator, no pots and pans, etc. etc. I’ve recently been experimenting with “dorm” cooking from the back of my math classroom (now that my final is over). We’ve made waffles (topped with marshmallows, Nutella, and dark chocolate) and some slightly questionable cold noodles. Please recommend simple recipes!! We have access to a power outlet, boiling water, and a microwave.
