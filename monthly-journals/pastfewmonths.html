Past few months...

Dear friends and family,

I’m back :>>>>

To be fully honest, I was so burnt out from writing (*cough cough* college apps *wheeze*) I needed a break from the empty page and blinking cursor.

But I’m back again, and as I take a few screen detox days on a school retreat, I’ve started to write again. This time, I’m writing Julia’s Journal on an actual piece of paper (!!!), to be transposed onto my computer once I have access to devices again.

Where to begin? I stopped writing in October, when I began the most diabolical lockin of my life on college apps. I guess I’ll try to summarize the past few months for you.

October

Fly-in program @ Caltech + stopped by SF/Berkeley on the way! After a string of rejections, getting accepted to a fly-in felt like a breath of fresh air. It was strange, and felt stranger still when I arrived on campus. My peers were National Champions in math, alums of MITES, ISEF winners, etc., etc. I felt achievement-less before realizing that I simply wasn’t sharing what I was proud of with the people I care about. I’ll do better!

November

End of golf season. I will always remember the team penguin-huddling under one umbrella as we attempted to escape the wind and rain at Jackson Park. Key word: attempted. I didn’t think our team could feel more team-like after saying goodbye to class of ’25 seniors, but it somehow did. Freshman year, I was choosing between golf and cheer (the closest thing to dance HNA had) – but looking back, I’m so glad I chose golf.

December

A month of ups and downs, but mostly ups.

Up: ate 6 tacos before getting full

Down: questioned life choices + crashed out over supps

Up: got into college!

Down: got scammed on vrbo :/

January

This month was kind of all over the place. Visited the Seattle Asian Art Museum for an APUSH project. Performed cultural dance at Physical Poetry. Received a letter from 2022 Julia. Opened it. Crode. And my last high school finals!!

February

Experienced post-Super-Bowl Seattle. Seattle freeze who? I’ve already told some of you this story, but I suddenly remembered that I met Russell Wilson when I was 7. Long story short: he visited my elementary school, offered to sign my water bottle, AND I SAID NO BECAUSE I DIDN’T KNOW WHO HE WAS. Generational fumble, I know.

Bear with me as I get used to writing again! As always, pleaseee respond to this newsletter! Can white chocolate be used to make hot chocolate? How many tacos can you eat in one sitting? I’ll be very impressed if you can beat my 6-taco record.

Lots of love,

Julia
